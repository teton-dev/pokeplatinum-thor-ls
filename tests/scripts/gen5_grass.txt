# Gen 5 battles, milestone 4b: a wild Pokémon from the tall grass, in Black 2 and White 2's engine because
# Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5 battles is on. From the verity-grass fixture (lake_grass.txt): into the grass at Lake
# Verity, back and forth until a wild Pokémon appears, then RUN until it works; passes once back on the overworld.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_grass.txt --from verity-grass

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
walk RIGHT 1
pace UP DOWN until battle timeout 9000
until command
wait 60
shot gen5-grass-command
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free
shot gen5-grass-back
exit 0
