# Gen 5 battles: Pokémon keep their moves (docs/thor/dev/gen5-battles.md). With a vanilla Platinum ROM the bridge's
# move map was all zero until 2026-11-14: BW2's battles got no moves (Struggle) and gave the party back none. Turtwig
# uses Tackle in BW2's engine, the battle ends, and Turtwig still has Tackle (33) and Withdraw (110), Tackle a PP down. The check is
# the `party` lines ("moves 33/34 110/40"). Gen 5 builds.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_keep_moves.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
party
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 128 95
wait 30
touch 64 54
# Bidoof faints or Turtwig runs: either way, back to the field
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
party
exit 0
