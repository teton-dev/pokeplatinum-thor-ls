# Gen 5 screens: field moves without HMs (Settings > Gameplay, on by default) in BW2's party list. The first Pokemon
# knows no field move; with the HM01 (Cut) in the bag it is offered Cut anyway (Fly is lent too: it can be used
# anywhere with its HM). From the turtwig fixture; here, with no HM in the bag, nothing is lent, so the test gives
# HM02 (Fly, item 421) first: Fly shows in the Pokemon's FIELD MOVES.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_party_hmfree.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
item 421 1
app party-field
wait 300
press A
wait 90
shot gen5-hmfree-menu
press DOWN
wait 10
press A
wait 90
shot gen5-hmfree-fly
exit 0
