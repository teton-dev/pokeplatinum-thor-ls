# Gen 5 battles, milestone 5: story trainers in Black 2 and White 2's engine with Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5 battles
# on: Gym Leader Roark (Platinum's trainer 246), then the rival on Route 201 (trainer 851), each started as its encounter

trainer 246

wait 120

until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30

wait 30

wait 120

shot gen5-rival-back
