# A battle starts in the weather the field is in (Platinum's does; the Battle Arcade's roulette sets one too): rain (2) and a
# sandstorm (10), in BW2's engine. The check is the log ("Gen 5: ... weather") and the shots: the weather's message at the
# start and its animation.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_weather.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
weather 2
wild 399 5
until battle
until command
wait 60
shot rain-command
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free
weather 10
wild 399 5
until battle
mash B for 200
shot sand-start
until command
wait 60
shot sand-command
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free
exit 0
