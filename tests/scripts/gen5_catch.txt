# Gen 5 battles, milestone 4c: catching in Black 2 and White 2's engine. From the turtwig fixture, with two Master
# Balls in the bag: a wild Bidoof caught into the party (the naming screen left empty), then, with the party filled
# to six, a wild Starly caught into the PC. Saved, and kept as the fixture gen5-caught, which gen5_caught.txt loads to
# check what the save has. The log's "party", "box" and "Pokedex" lines are the check.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_catch.txt --from turtwig --keep gen5-caught

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
item 1 2
item 4 5
party
# A Bidoof, into the party: BAG, POKE BALLS, the Master Ball, USE
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
mash B until naming timeout 6000
wait 90
shot gen5-catch-naming
# OK with no name: it keeps its species' name
press START
wait 30
press A
mash B until free
party
# Four more in the party (six), then a Starly, into the PC
give 403 3
give 396 3
give 399 3
give 401 3
wild 396 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
mash B until naming timeout 6000
wait 90
press START
wait 30
press A
mash B until free
shot gen5-catch-back
party
save
exit 0
