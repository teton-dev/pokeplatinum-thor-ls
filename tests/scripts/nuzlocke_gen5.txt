# Nuzlocke mode, milestone 4: its rules in Black 2 and White 2's engine (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md). From nuz-normal
# (Normal, crit guard; Roark's cap, 14), with Gen 5 battles on and Master Balls in the bag:
# - a Starly run from leaves the area to Starlys ("[test] nuzlocke: ... missed 396");
# - a Master Ball at a Bidoof there is knocked back ("Gen 5: a ball knocked back", the shot shows the message);
# - a level 14 Bidoof (at the cap) beats a wild one and gains no EXP (its EXP stays 2744 in the "[test] party" lines);
# - "Gen 5: level cap 14, crit guard" in the log: the battle got the cap and the crit guard.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_gen5.txt --from nuz-normal

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
item 1 5
wait 40
nuzlocke state
# The area's first encounter, a Starly: run
wild 396 3
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
nuzlocke state
# A Bidoof: BAG, POKE BALLS, the Master Ball, USE: knocked back
wild 399 3
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
wait 160
shot nuz-gen5-refused-1
wait 50
shot nuz-gen5-refused-2
wait 50
shot nuz-gen5-refused-3
wait 50
shot nuz-gen5-refused-4
until command timeout 3000
wait 60
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
party
# A level 14 Bidoof in front, Tackle first: FIGHT and Tackle (A, A) until the wild one faints
give 399 14
lead 1
learn 33
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
mash A until free timeout 9000
party
nuzlocke state
exit 0
