# Gen 5 battles: losing a battle in Black 2 and White 2's engine. From the turtwig fixture, a wild level 100
# Charizard, whose moves at that level all do damage, knocks Turtwig out in one turn; B through the messages (the
# money lost, the blackout) until the player is free on the overworld again. A loss read the money from a trainer
# card the port didn't have (NULL), and crashed after "... is out of usable Pokémon".
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_loss.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
mash A until free
gen5-battle 6 100
until battle
until command
wait 60
# FIGHT, then Tackle
touch 128 95
wait 30
touch 64 54
wait 120
shot gen5-loss-hit
mash B until free timeout 3600
shot gen5-loss-back
exit 0
