# Gen 5 battles, milestone 4b: a wild Pokémon met as in tall grass (the field's own wild encounter, not the debug
# battle), in Black 2 and White 2's engine because Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5 battles is on. From the turtwig fixture:
# a level 5 Bidoof, RUN until it works; passes once back on the overworld.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_wild.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
wild 399 5
until battle
until command
wait 60
shot gen5-wild-command
# RUN, at the bottom of the touch screen, until it works, with B through the messages
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free
shot gen5-wild-back
exit 0
