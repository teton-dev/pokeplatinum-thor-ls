# Widescreen: the screens after a catch in Black 2 and White 2's engine. A wild Bidoof (a new species) caught with a
# Master Ball, YES to the nickname question: Platinum's Pokedex entry, then its naming screen, fill the wide window
# (their layers' edge columns continued across the sides, the 3D Pokemon and the text in the 4:3 middle). The check is
# the shots. From the turtwig fixture:
#
#   TEST_CONFIG='SecondaryScreen=true Widescreen=true WindowWidth=768 WindowHeight=432' \
#       tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/widescreen_gen5_catch.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
item 1 2
# BAG, POKE BALLS, the Master Ball, USE
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
# The ball, the shakes, "Gotcha!", then YES to "Give a nickname to the caught ...?"
wait 1500
shot wide-question
mash touch 128 55 for 40
wait 600
shot wide-dex-entry
mash A until naming timeout 3000
wait 300
shot wide-naming
# OK with no name: it keeps its species' name
press START
wait 30
press A
mash B until free
shot wide-back
party
exit 0
