# Gen 5 battles: Lucas and Dawn as opponents, drawn with Platinum's own sprites (BW2's hero and heroine classes drew a
# black square, seen on the Thor). From the turtwig fixture: Dawn (trainer 616), then Lucas (613); a shot of each
# trainer sliding in, then the battle mashed to its end.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_lucas_dawn.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
give 395 50
lead 1
trainer 616
until battle
wait 150
shot dawn-intro
until command timeout 6000
mash A until free timeout 15000
trainer 613
until battle
wait 150
shot lucas-intro
until command timeout 6000
mash A until free timeout 15000
exit 0
