# Gen 5 battles: the nickname question after a catch ("Give a nickname to the caught ...?", BW2's Yes and No, as
# Platinum's battle asks it). From the turtwig fixture with a Master Ball: a wild Bidoof caught; the shot shows the
# question; B answers No, so no naming screen opens and the Bidoof joins the party with its species' name (the log's
# "a nickname for the catch: no" and "party" lines).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_nickname.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
item 1 1
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
wait 900
shot gen5-nickname-1
wait 600
shot gen5-nickname-2
wait 600
shot gen5-nickname-question
mash B until free timeout 6000
party
exit 0
