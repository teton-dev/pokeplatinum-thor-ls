# Nuzlocke mode: the starter must be named (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md). In Sandgem Lab, Prof. Rowan offers to
# nickname it; with the mode on, No asks again, then Yes opens the naming screen. The check is the `party` line
# ("nickname 1") and the shots.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_starter_name.txt --from nuz-normal
#
# From turtwig (the mode off) No is taken: the after-no shot shows the professor going on, and the run stops waiting
# for a naming screen that doesn't come.

mash A until free
nuzlocke state
# Into the lab at its door: the scene that gives the Pokédex starts (VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE is still 0)
jump 422 7 14
mash A until menu timeout 6000
wait 20
shot offer
press B
wait 30
shot after-no
# With the mode on, the question is up again: Yes; with it off, the scene goes on without naming
mash B until menu timeout 600
press A
mash A until naming timeout 600
wait 60
shot naming
# "SA", then OK
touch 160 118
wait 10
touch 34 99
wait 10
touch 216 70
# "You're happy with the nickname?": Yes, then the rest of the scene
mash A until free timeout 6000
party
exit 0
