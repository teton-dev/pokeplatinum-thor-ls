# Gen 5 battles: the catching tutorial (Route 202's: the counterpart catches a Bidoof with their starter), as BW2's
# capture demo plays it: Lucas or Dawn's back and name, their starter's first move, the Bidoof's Tackle, their line
# "OK! Its HP is down!..." and the throw ("... used a Poké Ball!"), then back to the overworld; nothing is kept.
# From the turtwig fixture (a girl's save would show Lucas).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_tutorial.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
party
tutorial
until battle
wait 400
shot gen5-tutorial-1
wait 300
shot gen5-tutorial-2
wait 300
shot gen5-tutorial-3
wait 300
shot gen5-tutorial-4
wait 300
shot gen5-tutorial-5
wait 300
shot gen5-tutorial-6
mash A until free timeout 6000
party
exit 0
