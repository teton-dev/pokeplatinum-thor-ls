# Nuzlocke mode: shinies are always catchable (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md). From nuz-normal (Normal), in the fixture's
# area (the player's house, location 1): a Starly run from leaves the area to Starlys; a shiny Bidoof (the test
# command `wild ... shiny`) is caught all the same and named, and the area still has its catch ("missed 396"). The
# check is the "[test] nuzlocke:" and "[test] party" lines and the shots.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_shiny.txt --from nuz-normal

mash A until free
gen5-battles off
item 4 10
item 1 5
wait 40
# The area's first encounter, a Starly: run from it
wild 396 3
until battle
until command
wait 60
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
nuzlocke state
# A shiny Bidoof: a Master Ball (the bag, the Poke Balls pocket, the Master Ball, Use) catches it
wild 399 3 shiny
until battle
until command
wait 60
shot shiny
touch 40 178
wait 90
touch 192 55
wait 60
touch 192 30
wait 60
touch 104 174
mash A until naming timeout 6000
wait 30
touch 160 118
wait 10
touch 216 70
mash B until free timeout 6000
party
nuzlocke state
exit 0
