# Widescreen: the evolution after a battle in Black 2 and White 2's engine. A level 6 Wurmple in the lead beats a
# wild level 4 Chansey (Tackle until it's over), grows to level 7 and evolves: Platinum's evolution scene fills the
# wide window (its layers' edge columns continued across the sides, the 3D Pokemon in the 4:3 middle). The check is
# the shots and the log's party (species 266 or 268 in slot 0). From the turtwig fixture:
#
#   TEST_CONFIG='SecondaryScreen=true Widescreen=true WindowWidth=768 WindowHeight=432' \
#       tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/widescreen_gen5_evolution.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
give 265 6
lead 1
wild 113 4
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30
# FIGHT, Tackle, until the battle is over and the evolution starts
mash A for 7000
shot wide-evolution-1
wait 200
shot wide-evolution-2
wait 200
shot wide-evolution-3
wait 200
shot wide-evolution-4
mash A until free timeout 12000
shot wide-back
party
exit 0
