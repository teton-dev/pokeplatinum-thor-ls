# Gen 5 battles: a roaming Pokémon (BATTLE_TYPE_ROAMER) in Black 2 and White 2's engine. From the turtwig fixture:
# Mesprit at level 50; Turtwig uses Tackle and Mesprit flees (BW2's roaming AI, BTL_SETUP_FLAG_ROAMING). The log's
# "the wild Pokémon after the battle" line has the HP it is left with (which RoamerAfterBattle_UpdateRoamers keeps).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_roamer.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
roamer 481 50
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
touch 128 95
wait 60
touch 64 54
wait 60
shot gen5-roamer
mash A until free timeout 6000
party
exit 0
