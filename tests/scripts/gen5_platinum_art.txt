# in a motion-capture suit, seen on the Thor). From the turtwig fixture: the Interviewers Roxy & Oli (883, a double

trainer 296
until battle
wait 150

until command timeout 6000
mash A until free timeout 15000
trainer 16
until battle
wait 150
shot camper
until command timeout 6000
mash A until free timeout 15000
