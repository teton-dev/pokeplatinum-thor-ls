# Gen 5 battles, milestone 5: a trainer battle in Black 2 and White 2's engine with Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5
# battles on. From the turtwig fixture: Youngster Tristan (Platinum's trainer 1, a level 5 Starly), started as his
# encounter starts it; A picks FIGHT and Tackle every turn until it's over. The log has the money won and the party
# after; the shots the battle and the overworld. Turtwig usually wins with about half its HP left (money +80); the
# damage rolls can make it lose (money -48), and the test passes either way: it checks the battle runs to the end.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_trainer.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
party
trainer 1
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30
shot gen5-trainer-command
mash A until free timeout 12000
shot gen5-trainer-back
party
exit 0
