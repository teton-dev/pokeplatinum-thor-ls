# Gen 5 battles, milestone 7: double battles in Black 2 and White 2's engine, from the turtwig fixture, with a level 50 Empoleon
# given for the player's second place. Three battles, each fought with A (FIGHT, the first move, the
# first target) until it's over:
#   - Twins Liv & Liz (trainer 15): one trainer, two Pokémon at once (Platinum's TRAINER_DOUBLES)
#   - Youngster Tristan (1) and Camper Jacob (16): two trainers, one Pokémon each at a time (TAG_DOUBLES; BW2's 1v2)
#   - the same two beside Cheryl (608): a partner whom BW2's AI plays (TRAINER_WITH_AI_PARTNER; BW2's 2v2)
# The log has each battle's setup line and the party after; the shots each battle's first command and the overworld.
# Losses pass too: the test checks the battles run to the end.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_doubles.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
give 395 50
party
trainer 15
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30
shot gen5-doubles-twins
mash A until free timeout 15000
shot gen5-doubles-twins-back
party
trainers 1 16
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30
shot gen5-doubles-two
mash A until free timeout 15000
shot gen5-doubles-two-back
party
trainers 1 16 608
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 30
shot gen5-doubles-partner
mash A until free timeout 15000
shot gen5-doubles-partner-back
party
exit 0
