# Gen 5 battles: a legendary's scripted encounter (BATTLE_STATUS_LEGENDARY) in Black 2 and White 2's engine. From the
# turtwig fixture with a Master Ball: Uxie at level 50 (BW2's message has no "wild", BTL_SETUP_FLAG_LEGENDARY), caught
# at once (BAG, POKE BALLS, the Master Ball, USE), its naming screen left empty: the log's party has it.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_legendary.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
item 1 1
legendary 480 50
until battle
wait 200
shot gen5-legendary-intro
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
touch 40 180
wait 60
touch 192 48
wait 60
touch 64 33
wait 60
touch 108 175
# YES to "Give a nickname to the caught ...?" (BW2's Yes and No on the touch screen)
mash A touch 128 55 until naming timeout 6000
wait 90
press START
wait 30
press A
mash B until free
party
exit 0
