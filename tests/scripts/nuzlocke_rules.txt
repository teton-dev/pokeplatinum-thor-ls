# Nuzlocke mode, milestone 2: the rules in Platinum's own engine (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md). From nuz-normal
# (Normal, crit guard; Roark is next, so the level cap is his strongest Pokemon's, 14). The check is the
# "[test] party" lines:
# - a Bidoof and a Garchomp given with the mode on have IVs of 15 (Turtwig, made before, keeps its own);
# - Turtwig set to 0 HP (as a battle's party update writes it) is dead;
# - Bidoof, at the cap (level 14, its EXP 2744), beats a wild level 2 Bidoof and gains no EXP;
# - a Pokemon Center's nurse then heals Bidoof but leaves Turtwig at 0 HP.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_rules.txt --from nuz-normal

mash A until free
gen5-battles off
nuzlocke state
give 445 100
give 399 14
faint 0
party
# Bidoof to the front, with Tackle first; FIGHT and Tackle (A, A) until the wild Bidoof faints
lead 2
learn 33
wild 399 2
until battle
until command
mash A until free timeout 9000
party
# A Pokemon Center's nurse (as heal_shots.txt reaches her)
jump 6 8 5
wait 120
mash A until free
press UP
wait 10
press A
mash A for 900
mash A until free timeout 6000
party
nuzlocke state
exit 0
