# Gen 5 screens: field moves in BW2's party list. The first Pokemon is taught Cut, the party opened as the start menu
# opens it (app party-field), and Cut chosen from the Pokemon's FIELD MOVES: where the player stands there is nothing to
# cut, so Platinum's check answers "can't use that here" through BW2's message. From the turtwig fixture:
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_party_moves.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
teach 15 2
app party-field
wait 300
press A
wait 90
press DOWN
wait 10
press A
wait 90
shot gen5-moves-list
press DOWN 2
wait 10
shot gen5-moves-cursor
press A
wait 120
shot gen5-moves-cut
exit 0
