# Gen 5 battles, the Safari Zone: a catch. From the turtwig fixture: a wild Caterpie (catch rate 255: a Safari Ball always
# catches it) with 12 balls; the naming screen is left empty and the Pokémon goes to the party. The log's "party" and
# "Pokedex" lines are the check (the Caterpie caught, 11 balls left in the log's "battle ended" line).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_safari_catch.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
party
safari 10 5 12
until battle
until command
wait 90
# BALL
touch 128 80
mash B until naming timeout 6000
wait 90
shot gen5-safari-naming
press START
wait 30
press A
mash B until free
party
shot gen5-safari-caught
exit 0
