# Gen 5 battles, milestone 5: Gym Leader Roark's real script in Black 2 and White 2's engine, as a player meets him:
# into Oreburgh Gym (map 47) in front of Roark, A, his lines, the battle (Tackle every turn: Turtwig loses to his level
# 12 Pokémon), then the script's loss path: the blackout. The log has the battle's result and the money; the shots the
# battle's start and where the player wakes. From the turtwig fixture:
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_roark.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
gen5-battles on
mash A until free
party
jump 47 5 6
until free timeout 3000
walk UP 2
press A
mash A until battle timeout 3000
mash A until command timeout 6000
shot gen5-roark-command
# The battle, then the blackout and the Pokémon Center
mash A until free timeout 20000
shot gen5-roark-after
party
exit 0
