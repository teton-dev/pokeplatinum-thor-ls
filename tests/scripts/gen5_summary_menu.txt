# Gen 5 screens: the Summary from Platinum's own start menu, as the player gets there. From the turtwig fixture: X opens
# the menu, POKEMON (the first entry) the party, A on Turtwig and SUMMARY open BW2's summary, B goes back to the party
# (the cursor still on Turtwig), B and B again to the overworld, where the player is free.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_summary_menu.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
press X
wait 60
shot menu
press A
wait 200
shot party
press A
wait 60
shot party-menu
press A
wait 200
shot summary
press B
wait 200
shot back-in-party
press B
wait 120
press B
wait 200
until free
shot field
exit 0
