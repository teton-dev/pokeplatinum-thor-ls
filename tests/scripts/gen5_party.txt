# Gen 5 screens: the party in Black 2 and White 2's own list (Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5 screens). From the turtwig
# fixture: opens the party over the overworld (test command app party-field: the start menu's), shoots it, opens the
# Pokemon's menu with A, then its summary with A, and shoots each. The run ends in the app.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_party.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
app party-field
wait 300
shot gen5-party
press A
wait 120
shot gen5-party-menu
press A
wait 300
shot gen5-party-summary
exit 0
