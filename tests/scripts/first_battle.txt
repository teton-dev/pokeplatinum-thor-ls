# The first battle: from the before-starter fixture (new_game.txt), Turtwig from Prof. Rowan's briefcase, then
# Barry's challenge, until the battle asks what to do. Passes when the battle has loaded and is waiting for the
# player: the check that a battle engine (Platinum's, or Gen 5's with Gen 5 battles on) starts and runs.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/first_battle.txt --from before-starter

# The title screen and Continue: back on Route 201, two tiles right of the briefcase
mash A until free
walk LEFT 1

# The briefcase opens the starter choice. Left as far as it goes is Turtwig; A, then Yes
press A
mash LEFT for 300
mash A until menu
shot starter
press A

# Barry chooses his and challenges the player
mash A until battle
mash A until command
wait 30
shot battle
exit 0
