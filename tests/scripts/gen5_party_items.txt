# Gen 5 screens: giving and taking held items in BW2's party list. From the turtwig fixture: three Potions in the bag, the
# party opened as the bag opens it to give one (test command app party-give: pick the Pokemon), Turtwig picked, then the
# party opened as the start menu opens it (app party-field), ITEM > TAKE from Turtwig. The log's lines (party) show the
# held item and the Potions in the bag before, after the give (item 17, 2 Potions left) and after the take (3 again).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_party_items.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
item 17 3
party
app party-give
wait 300
shot gen5-give-list
press A
wait 200
shot gen5-give-done
mash B for 120
wait 60
party
exit 0
