# Nuzlocke mode: items (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md). From nuz-normal (Normal; the next leader is Roark, whose strongest
# Pokémon is level 14 and weakest 12):
# - Rare Candy (the test command `use`, as the party menu checks and applies an item) does nothing at the cap and
#   raises a level 13 Pokémon to it, then nothing;
# - a dead Pokémon takes no Potion and no Revive;
# - with Hard the cap is 12;
# - Roark's two Potions are taken ("Nuzlocke mode: trainer 246's 2 items taken");
# - the bag in battle has nothing but Poké Balls: Platinum's other pockets are empty (its HP/PP RESTORE shot), and
#   so is BW2's (its gen5-bag-hp-pp shot; Gen 5 builds).
# The check is the log's lines and the shots.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_items.txt --from nuz-normal

gen5-import
mash A until free
gen5-battles off
item 4 10
item 17 5
item 28 2
item 50 3
nuzlocke state
# Rare Candy: refused at the cap (14), taken below it
give 399 14
use 50 1
give 399 13
use 50 2
use 50 2
party
# A dead Pokémon: no Potion, no Revive
faint 2
use 17 2
use 28 2
party
# Hard: the cap is the leader's weakest Pokémon's level
nuzlocke hard
wait 2
nuzlocke state
# BW2's bag in a wild battle: only the Balls pocket
gen5-battles on
wild 396 3
until battle
until command
wait 90
touch 40 180
wait 90
shot gen5-bag
touch 64 48
wait 60
shot gen5-bag-hp-pp
mash B for 90
wait 30
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
gen5-battles off
# Roark, in Platinum's engine: his items taken; the bag's HP/PP RESTORE pocket is empty although there are Potions
trainer 246
until battle
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
touch 40 178
wait 90
shot bag
touch 64 55
wait 60
shot bag-hp-pp
exit 0
