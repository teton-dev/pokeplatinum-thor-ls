# Gen 5 battles: winning a battle in Black 2 and White 2's engine, and what it changes in the party. From the turtwig
# fixture, a wild level 1 Pichu, which Turtwig's Tackle knocks out (its least damage is about Pichu's HP); B through
# the experience messages until the player is free on the overworld again. The log's "party 0 after the battle" line
# has Turtwig's experience, which went up (204 in the fixture).
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_win.txt --from turtwig

gen5-import
mash A until free
gen5-battle 172 1
until battle
until command
wait 60
# FIGHT, then Tackle
touch 128 95
wait 30
touch 64 54
wait 120
shot gen5-win-hit
mash B until free timeout 3600
shot gen5-win-back
exit 0
