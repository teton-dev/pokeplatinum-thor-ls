# Widescreen on BW2's Summary screen (Settings > Gameplay > Gen 5 screens): the info page, the stats page (RIGHT), the
# moves page, a move's details (A on a move), the ribbons page. From the turtwig fixture:
#
#   TEST_CONFIG='SecondaryScreen=true Widescreen=true WindowWidth=768 WindowHeight=432' \
#       tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/widescreen_gen5_summary.txt --from turtwig
gen5-import
gen5-screens on
mash A until free
wait 30
app summary
wait 300
shot summary-info
press RIGHT
wait 120
shot summary-stats
press RIGHT
wait 120
shot summary-moves
press A
wait 120
shot summary-move-detail
press B
wait 60
press RIGHT
wait 120
shot summary-ribbons
exit 0
